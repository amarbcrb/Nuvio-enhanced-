Supported
  1. Looks up the English title, original title and year from the TMDB ID.
  2. Gets a guest token from MovieBox (x-user header).
  3. Searches by title and keeps exact matches with a close year. Each dub, like Title [Hindi], is its own entry.
  4. Collects MP4 and subtitles from subject/download and HLS or DASH from subject/play. VIP-only sources are skipped.
